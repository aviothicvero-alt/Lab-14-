# Lab-14-
Here is a concise, executive summary describing Day 14 — Lab 14: Capstone GenAI Workflow Portfolio Presentation:

Overview
This capstone project serves as the cumulative synthesis of the course, requiring students to design, audit, document, and present an end-to-end, human-in-the-loop Generative AI workflow for a real-world aviation business challenge (such as flight disruption management, passenger complaint handling, or operational policy navigation).

Key Highlights
End-to-End Workflow Architecture: Maps the complete lifecycle of AI integration from initial business input and data sourcing through tool selection, prompt engineering, content generation, and multi-layered review gates before final operational deployment.

Multi-Tool Matrix: Justifies the tactical selection of specific Generative AI platforms (ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Canva, Gamma, image-generation, and document-grounded AI tools) matched precisely to required text, visual, or presentation outputs and their associated operational risk levels.

Rigorous Review & Verification Audit: Mandates strict quality control across every output using:

Four-Dimension Review Gate: Audits text outputs for Accuracy, Tone, Compliance, and Context.

Hallucination & Privacy Audits: Identifies and redacts any unverified claims or sensitive personal data (e.g., PII like Aadhaar, passport, or booking details) using generic placeholders.

Verification Trail & Triage: Validates facts against official sources using a Claim → Source → Match → Context → Action protocol and triages risks via a Severity × Risk matrix.

Structured Portfolio & Capstone Presentation: Packages all deliverables—including an iterative 5-part prompt portfolio, workplace communications, visual assets, 8–12 slide presentation deck, and individual reflections—into a standardized, public-ready GitHub repository (day-14-capstone-portfolio).

Human-in-the-Loop Imperative: Reaffirms that Generative AI functions exclusively as a drafting and analytical assistant, reserving final decision-making, policy verification, and operational approval strictly for human aviation professionals.
